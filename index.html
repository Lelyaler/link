A signal is any discreet event entering your infrastructure — such as a Stripe payment webhook, an IoT telemetry ping, an updated CRM record in Salesforce, or an API call from your internal backend services. LinkFlow normalizes these heterogeneous payloads into clean, actionable data streams in real time.
