Company & Philosophy

We are building the substrate for autonomous data flow

Founded by distributed systems engineers and machine learning researchers frustrated by fragile integrations.

For decades, connecting enterprise software meant choosing between two extremes: rigid, slow point-to-point Zapier-style triggers that break when schemas change, or bespoke Python scripts and cron jobs that require round-the-clock developer maintenance.

In 2024, our founding team set out to engineer **LinkFlow** — a platform where incoming signals are treated as first-class citizens. By integrating calibrated AI decision agents directly into the data path, LinkFlow allows engineering teams to automate complex business operations with guaranteed sub-8ms latency.

01

Zero Glue Code

Software engineers should build core product value, not waste weeks maintaining brittle webhooks, retry loops, and webhook signing verifications.

02

Deterministic AI

We reject hallucinations. Our calibrated agents enforce strict typed schemas, confidence boundaries, and automated fallback rules for mission-critical operations.

03

Radical Privacy

Your data belongs exclusively to you. LinkFlow processes payloads in transient cryptographic memory envelopes with absolute zero-retention defaults.
